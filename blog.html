Why the relay never touches your screen

Engineering note

Syncropy's pairing relay (relay-worker/, a Cloudflare Durable Object) is deliberately a dumb pipe: it forwards whatever WebSocket messages two paired peers send each other and never inspects their content. For the screen-pairing demo at /connect.html, that means the relay only ever sees a WebRTC signaling handshake (SDP offer/answer, ICE candidates) - the actual screen share travels peer-to-peer between the two browsers once that handshake completes, over an encrypted WebRTC media channel the relay is never part of.

There's no shared server secret for the public pairing path either: a room is just a high-entropy, client-generated code, kept in its own Durable Object namespace so it can never collide with the separately-authenticated /relay/:room path. That's what "zero-trust" means here in concrete terms, not a marketing label - see the relay's own source for the exact routing.

Honest current limits, in case you're evaluating this: there's no TURN relay fallback yet (pairing across two strict/symmetric NATs can fail to find a direct path), and remote control - keyboard/mouse input, not just viewing - isn't built. Both are real, specific next steps, not vague roadmap items.
